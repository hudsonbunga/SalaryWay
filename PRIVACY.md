# SalaryWay – Privacy Policy & Data Handling

**Tagline**: *Give Every Rupee a Direction.*

## Core Privacy Principle
**Your financial data belongs exclusively to you.** SalaryWay does not upload your transactions, salaries, debts, or savings goals to remote tracking servers.

## What Data is Stored
1. **Salary and Allocation Configuration**: Your monthly income and chosen percentage breakdown across the 5 categories (Essential, Lifestyle, Debt, Savings, Invest in Myself).
2. **Transaction Ledger**: Manually entered or demo-imported transaction records (title, amount, date, category, payment method).
3. **Debts & Goals**: Outstanding balances, loan EMIs, and savings target milestones.

## Where Data is Stored
- **100% On-Device**: Stored locally in the private app sandbox.
- **Data Deletion**: Tapping "Reset to Demo Data" or clearing the application storage completely wipes all user data immediately.

## Bank Connectivity Notice
The current version operates in a local demonstration mode compliant with RBI Account Aggregator specifications. No live banking credentials, SMS logs, or banking app screens are ever accessed or scraped.
